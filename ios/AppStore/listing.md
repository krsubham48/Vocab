## Description (4000)

```text
Vocab helps you learn English words that turn up in newspapers, magazines, TV and conversation, one word at a time.

TWO DECKS
• Everyday: 294 words from the news and daily life.
• Advanced: 1,018 richer words for confident reading and writing.
Every word has a simple pronunciation guide, a clear meaning and an example sentence.

HOW IT WORKS
• Learn: get a new word, and mark it once you know it.
• Revise: test yourself with flashcards made from your learnt words.
• Revisit: browse and search every word you have learnt.
• Calendar: see which days you learnt something, and what.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• Works completely offline.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Vocab collects no data.
• Made for iPhone and iPad, and follows your text size setting.
```
