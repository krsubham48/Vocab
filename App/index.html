About Vocab

Vocab helps you learn English words that turn up in newspapers, magazines, TV and conversation.

📖 Learn a new word

  1. Choose the Everyday or Advanced deck at the top.
  2. Tap New Word.
  3. Read the pronunciation, meaning and example sentence.
  4. Once you know it, tap Mark as Learnt. If you don't, it can come up again later.

🗂️ Revise with flashcards

  1. Read the word and try to recall its meaning.
  2. Tap the card to check.
  3. Tap Got it or Still learning to see the next card. Neither changes your learnt words.

📔 Revisit your words

  1. Scroll the list, or search by word or meaning.
  2. Tap a word to open its card, and tap it again to close it.
  3. To un-learn a word, open it, tap Remove and confirm.

📅 Calendar

  1. A dot marks each day this month when you learnt something.
  2. Tap a date to see that day's words.

📱 Good to know

  • Revise, Revisit and Calendar show only words you've marked as learnt.
  • To install on iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Once installed, it works offline. To get updates, open it while online, wait a few seconds, then close and reopen it.
  • Your progress is stored only on this device. Removing the app or clearing Safari data can erase it.
