Common questions

Where is my progress kept?

Only on your device. The iPhone and iPad app and the web app keep separate progress.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone or iPad?

Setting up the new device from a backup of the old one brings your progress with it. There is no other way to export it yet.

Why can't I see a word in Revise or Revisit?

They show only words you have marked as learnt.

How do I remove a learnt word?

In Revisit, tap the word, tap Remove, then confirm.
