Vocab privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026. Covers Vocab for iPhone and iPad and the Vocab web app.

What Vocab collects

Nothing. Vocab has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. Nothing about you or how you use it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your progress

The words you have learnt, and the dates you learnt them, are saved only on your device (for the web app, in your browser's storage). If you back up your device, the backup includes your progress and is handled by your backup provider, for example Apple's iCloud.

Deleting it

Delete the app, or clear the website's data in your browser, and your progress is erased from the device. We never receive it, so there is nothing for us to share or delete.

This website

These pages and the web app are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical details, such as your IP address, when you visit, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Vocab collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page. For questions, see help and contact.
